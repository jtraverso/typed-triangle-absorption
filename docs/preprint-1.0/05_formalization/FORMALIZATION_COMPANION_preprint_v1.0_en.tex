\subsection{1. Validated scope}\label{validated-scope}

Three explicit targets were compiled in the aligned campaign: \texttt{Partitioned\allowbreak{}T\allowbreak{}riangle\allowbreak{}Absor\allowbreak{}ption.\allowbreak{}Paper\allowbreak{}I\allowbreak{}I.\allowbreak{}Core}, \texttt{Partitioned\allowbreak{}T\allowbreak{}riangle\allowbreak{}Absor\allowbreak{}ption.\allowbreak{}Paper\allowbreak{}I\allowbreak{}I.\allowbreak{}Families}, and \texttt{Partitioned\allowbreak{}T\allowbreak{}riangle\allowbreak{}Absor\allowbreak{}ption.\allowbreak{}Paper\allowbreak{}I\allowbreak{}I.\allowbreak{}Paper\allowbreak{}IAppl\allowbreak{}ications}. Their overlapping import closures contain 1,510 project modules. The campaign began with an empty project output directory and compiled every module from source; 623 were compiled in the Paper I phase and 887 in the Paper II phase. The pinned Mathlib cache was reused. The fifteen final axiom queries passed with only \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.\allowbreak{}ch\allowbreak{}oice} and \texttt{Quot.\allowbreak{}sound}.

The result includes actual existence certificates for the seventeen conserved families. It also includes the exhaustive census, the bridge from representatives to the construction library and transport back to the original labelling. Together these prove the typical-host property for every family of three-element multisets on four labels that uses every label and gives every label a forbidden partner. Neither a Latin producer nor membership in the catalogue is assumed in the final theorem.

The completed local campaign is recorded as \texttt{PASS\_\allowbreak{}CLEAN\_\allowbreak{}A\allowbreak{}LIGNED\_\allowbreak{}BUILD\allowbreak{}}; it is not an independent reproduction. Revision v2 subsequently corrects author headers only and has status \texttt{PASS\_\allowbreak{}HEADER\_\allowbreak{}\allowbreak{}ONLY\_\allowbreak{}REVISIO\allowbreak{}N}; selected revision v4 has status \texttt{PASS\_\allowbreak{}README\_\allowbreak{}\allowbreak{}ONLY\_\allowbreak{}REVISIO\allowbreak{}N} and changes only the archive README and its manifest. Every proof-body byte is preserved. Historical receipts describe the sources before that correction, and the reversible verification in Section 5 reconstructs their exact bytes. No new Lean compilation is claimed for the corrected headers.

\subsection{4. Census and conserved families}\label{census-and-conserved-families}

The finite proof uses a reversible encoding of twenty triangle types and twenty-six boxes determined by forbidden partners. Coverage certificates provide actual permutations of labels. Minimality proves uniqueness of the representative. \texttt{census\_\allowbreak{}compl\allowbreak{}ete} combines these facts into an exact classification with 765 representatives.

The construction predicate need not itself be invariant under every permutation. The final proof transports the established typical-host property along the inverse permutation; it does not infer catalogue membership for the original labelling. All 28 bridge modules compiled in the complete closure.

For the conserved families, the proof supplies core and non-core certificates at a common scale before applying the splitting transfer. The physical reserve and catalogue are fixed before the residue. Weights count all catalogue occurrences of each arc, and the degree bound concerns total directed degree. Python generated candidate certificates; Lean checked coverage, uniqueness and transfer. The census bridge uses no \texttt{native\_\allowbreak{}decid\allowbreak{}e} or new mathematical axiom.

\subsection{6. Written proof and formal correspondence}\label{written-proof-and-formal-correspondence}

The mathematical appendices of Paper II now integrate the six written-proof dossiers at repository commit \texttt{61f752fc\allowbreak{}87bf\allowbreak{}f6f5\allowbreak{}654725b2\allowbreak{}\allowbreak{}3ce10ad4\allowbreak{}3023\allowbreak{}64ab\allowbreak{}}. Their source correspondence is listed in Section 7. The authoring operation preserves the theorem statements and frozen sources. It adds exposition; it does not infer mathematical validity from typesetting or finite arithmetic checks.

The correspondence has two levels. The declarations in Section 3 state the principal results. The construction modules in Section 7 supply the real inverse, signed certificates, refiner, semidense geometry, component balancing and conserved catalogue. Auxiliary ordinary-refiner, robust-matching, packing and cover-down results remain explicit dependencies. The finite family tables are also required by the universal classification: example components are not a substitute for those tables.

The semidense theorem, the balanced F1--F3 deduction and Lemma SC are distinct. In the semidense interface, ordered disagreement is twice the manuscript's unordered edit count. In the conserved catalogue, degree means total in-degree plus out-degree and the load sums over all catalogue members. Vertex splitting can change the order of the host, so relative supply and degree budgets are rescaled before projection. These conventions are material to the correspondence.

The manuscript, all mathematical appendices and auxiliary statements and this companion are available in full in English and Spanish. Both editions retain the same mathematical expressions, declaration identifiers and source hashes. The manuscript and this companion constitute their first public edition, preprint 1.0.

The selected archive documents the completed aligned campaign and its subsequent header-only and README-only revisions. Project sources and original packaging materials use Apache-2.0; dependency licences remain intact. The manuscript and this companion use CC BY 4.0. The recorded compilation is one local campaign, not independent reproduction.
